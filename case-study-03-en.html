Cross-Department Project

Migrating data from Software A to Software B

Ensuring a safe migration – continuous information – stable operations on the new system.

Timeline07/2025 – 09/2025
RoleProject Lead
ScopeCross-department
01 — Start with why

Why this project?

A company-wide migration to a new operating software.

Switching Operating Software

The company was moving to new software to optimize performance and user experience.

Preserving Operational Data

Keeping all historical data so operations, analysis, and reporting could continue without redoing any work.

02 — The core idea

Migration isn't just about moving data. It's about making sure operations aren't disrupted when the system changes.

03 — Results & impact

Results that created tangible value for the organization

3M+ records (~97%)
Successfully Migrated

Historical data was preserved fully and accurately.

Uninterrupted Operations

Operations continued without disruption throughout the migration.

Stable Operational Reporting

The reporting system kept running smoothly on the new data source.

04 — What I learned

Lessons from this project

The success of a migration isn't measured by how much data gets moved — it's measured by users never noticing the change at all.

Understand Right – Define Right

A deep understanding of operations is the foundation for correctly deciding what data to keep and what to improve.

Detailed Planning – Minimal Risk

A clear migration plan keeps risk under control and keeps you ahead of every situation.

Communicate. Communicate. Communicate.

Clear communication is the key to running a project effectively and driving it to success.
