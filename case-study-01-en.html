Building a Management Reporting System

From scattered data to a unified management view

Timeline2022 – Present
RoleData Lead
ScopeCompany-wide
View demo report
01 — Start with why

Why this project?

Three core problems made management difficult and called for a new reporting system.

Scattered Data

Data existed only at a granular level, with no centralized view aligned to the org chart.

Inconsistent KPIs

Each department defined KPIs differently, with no alignment or formal documentation.

Manual Reporting

Manual, repetitive data consolidation that was time-consuming and error-prone.

02 — The core idea

Don't start with the report.
Start with the goal.

An effective management reporting system starts with understanding the goal, aligning on how to measure it, and creating a continuous review loop to support decisions.

Periodic Review
01

Set goals at the start of the year

Review the prior year, combine leadership direction with departmental plans, and finalize the year's goals.

02

Gather management questions & align on data definitions

Identify the management questions to answer, and align on KPI definitions, units, calculation rules, data sources, and ownership.

03

Collect data

Collect and process target data (allocated from the annual goal) and actual data (recorded continuously) per the agreed definitions.

04

Build the report

Design and deploy a report that fully answers the management questions, tracks performance, and supports decisions.

05

Review progress periodically

Compare actuals against targets, assess trends, and act promptly. When context changes, the loop can return to step 1 or 2.

03 — Results & impact

Results that created real value for the organization

83%

Cut report compilation time.
From 60 minutes down to 10.

Standardized KPIs & Definitions

Created a shared data language across departments.

Automated, Timely Reporting

Information stays current and ready whenever needed.

Org-Wide Visibility

Leadership gains a complete view, enabling faster decisions.

04 — What I learned

Lessons from this project

Building a management reporting system doesn't start with a tool or a dashboard. It starts with understanding the goal, asking the right questions, and creating a shared data language.

Once those are in place, reporting becomes part of the management loop — from goal-setting, to tracking, to review, to action.
