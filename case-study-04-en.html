01 — Start with why

Why this project?

This reflects the company's broader mission: Base.vn builds business operating software that generates data — and businesses need to put that data to good use.

Helping Businesses Unlock Diverse Data

Operating software helps businesses manage work, customers, finances, HR, and more — generating large volumes of data along the way.

The Need for a Complete Picture

Businesses need a full, accurate, and timely view to understand what's really going on.

Deciding on Data

When data is organized the right way, decision-makers can act with more confidence and effectiveness.
