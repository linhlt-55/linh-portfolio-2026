01 — Start with why

Why this project?

To build an operations reporting system that gives stakeholders a shared view, surfaces problems early, and enables timely action.

No Shared View

Between the people monitoring operations and the people running them.

Problems Surfaced Too Late

Only noticed once they had already caused real consequences.

No Way to Spot Hotspots

Making it hard to act in time.

02 — The core idea

Operations reporting isn't just about seeing problems — it's about seeing them early enough to act.
